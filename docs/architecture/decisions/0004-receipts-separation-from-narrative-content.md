# 0004 - Receipts Separation from Narrative Content

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-02-26

## Context

Case studies and blog posts need to stay readable. Embedding full source lists in every narrative risks turning content into a link farm.

## Decision

Keep detailed receipts and source grouping in methods/research structures, and link narrative content by receipt type/tags rather than inline source-heavy blocks.

## Consequences

- Pros:
  - Preserves narrative readability.
  - Centralizes evidence handling and updates.
  - Supports reuse of evidence across multiple posts/cases.
- Cons:
  - Readers may need one extra click for full source depth.
  - Requires disciplined tagging and receipt maintenance.
