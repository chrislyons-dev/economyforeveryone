# Procurement and Contract Guardrails

**Use when:** the real leverage is buried in an RFP, vendor renewal, or contract amendment.  
**Goal:** get boring guardrails into the contract before failure becomes normal.

## Why this matters

A lot of public harm is not just bad policy.
It is weak vendor terms, vague metrics, or a renewal process that assumes performance instead of proving it.

Contracts decide:

- what gets measured
- what gets published
- what counts as failure
- what happens if the vendor underdelivers

## What good looks like

One contract ask that adds one or more of these:

- performance metrics
- audit rights
- data transparency
- cure periods and escalation steps
- termination for non-performance

## Quick targets

Pull these first:

- current contract or renewal packet
- RFP or procurement notice
- board packet or approval memo
- vendor scorecard if one exists
- next renewal or amendment date

## Core questions

- What is the vendor required to deliver?
- What metric proves they delivered it?
- Who can inspect the data?
- What happens if the vendor misses the mark?
- When is the next real decision point?

## Contract clauses worth asking for

### Audit clause

The public entity can inspect records, performance data, and billing documentation on a defined schedule.

### Performance metrics

The contract names 3-5 measurable outcomes, not just activity counts.

### Data transparency requirement

The vendor must provide plainly usable reporting on a set cadence.

### Cure and termination terms

If the vendor fails, the contract requires a correction plan and preserves the right to terminate for repeated non-performance.

## Decision rules

- If you cannot get a big rewrite, ask for reporting and audit rights first.
- If the contract is already signed, target the renewal, amendment, or oversight review.
- If staff say the terms are boilerplate, ask who controls the boilerplate.

## Scoreboard

- Contract located? (Y/N)
- Performance metrics present? (Y/N)
- Audit clause present? (Y/N)
- Public reporting requirement present? (Y/N)
- Next renewal or amendment date identified? (date)

## Definition of done

Leave with:

1. one contract guardrail ask
2. one target map or owner list
3. one follow-up date
4. one log entry
