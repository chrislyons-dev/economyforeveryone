**Use when:** elections matter and you want leverage without burning out.  
**Goal:** small team, simple rhythm, consistent turnout.

## Roles (lightweight)

- Captain: keeps the rhythm, runs the 15-min weekly check-in
- List keeper: tracks who needs what (rides, reminders, questions)
- Driver: rides / logistics
- Texter: sends reminders and scripts
- Backup: covers when life happens

## Weekly rhythm (15 minutes)

1. What election/date are we targeting?
2. Who are our 25–50 voters (friends/family/neighbors)?
3. Who needs a plan this week?
4. Who needs help removing friction?
5. Assign 3–5 touches. Done.

## The rule

Small and consistent beats big and frantic.

## What success looks like

A bunch of people vote who otherwise might not have.
No one feels lectured.
