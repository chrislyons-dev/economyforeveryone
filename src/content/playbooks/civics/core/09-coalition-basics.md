# Coalition Basics (Work with imperfect allies)

**Use when:** you need allies to move a thing and you don’t agree on everything.  
**Goal:** align on a shared goal, shared facts, shared rules.

## The minimum agreement

1. **Shared goal:** what we’re trying to achieve
2. **Shared facts:** what we agree is true (and what’s unknown)
3. **Shared rules:** how we treat each other and make decisions

## Practical coalition rules

- No purity tests
- No surprise press releases
- Disagree in private first
- Keep meetings short and action-oriented
- Name an exit rule (how to part without betrayal narratives)

## The “two yeses” decision rule (simple)

- We need **yes** on the goal
- We need **yes** on the next step
  Not yes on every belief.

## Watchouts

- Identity fights replace the mission
- Vague goals = endless meetings
- “All downside, no plan” energy

## What success looks like

A coalition that ships: meeting → decision → action → follow-up.
