# Voting Plan (Personal + Household)

**Use when:** it’s 60 days out (or whenever you realize you’re behind).  
**Goal:** remove friction so voting is boring and guaranteed.

## The plan (15 minutes)

1. Confirm registration (you + household)
2. Decide voting method (early, mail, day-of)
3. Pick the date/time and put it on the calendar
4. Remove the blocker (ID, ride, childcare, time off)
5. Get the ballot info (sample ballot, key races)
6. Make a backup plan (if work/kids chaos hits)

## “What could stop me?”

- Work schedule → choose early/mail
- Transportation → line up a ride / transit plan
- Childcare → trade with a friend for 2 hours
- Forgetting → 2 reminders (a week before + the day before)

## Keep it human

Voting isn’t your whole identity.
It’s one habit that protects everything else.

## What success looks like

You voted, calmly.
You helped one other person do the same.
