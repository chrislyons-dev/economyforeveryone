**Use when:** you donate and want impact, not vibes.  
**Goal:** money as a tool for outcomes, not a mood.

## The giving budget (guardrail)

- Decide a monthly amount you can sustain
- Prefer recurring giving (stability beats spikes)

## Where money tends to matter (often)

- Local turnout and voter protection
- Legal defense / due process support (credible orgs)
- Watchdog journalism and transparency orgs
- Implementation capacity (where allowed): staffing, training, tooling
- Community mutual aid funds (with accountability)

## Your giving rubric (pick 3)

1. Clear measurable outcomes
2. Transparent reporting
3. Local leverage
4. Track record of shipping
5. Low drama / high execution

## Avoid (usually)

- Pure outrage fundraising with no plan
- Org sprawl you can’t track
- “One weird trick” promises

## What success looks like

You can explain why you donate in one sentence and it still sounds sane.
