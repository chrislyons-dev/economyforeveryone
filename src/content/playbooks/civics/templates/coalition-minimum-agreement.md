# Coalition Minimum Agreement

Use this when a small coalition needs enough structure to work together without pretending everyone agrees on everything.

## Shared goal

We are working together to move this one ask:

- [ask]

## Shared facts

We agree on these facts or evidence points:

- [fact]
- [fact]
- [fact]

## Shared rules

- We focus on the ask, not each other’s whole worldview.
- We do not make claims we cannot support.
- We name disagreements early and keep them narrow.
- We do not publicly surprise each other on the core ask.

## Shared timeline

- next decision date:
- next action:
- next follow-up:

## Exit rule

If the coalition can no longer support the shared ask or shared rules, any group may step back without public drama.
