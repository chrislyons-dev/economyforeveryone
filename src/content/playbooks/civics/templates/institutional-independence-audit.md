# Institutional Independence Audit

Copy-pasteable question set for evaluating one oversight body: an inspector general, redistricting commission, election authority, ethics office, or public broadcaster board.

Use with [Accountability (Watchdog Lite)](/playbooks/civics/core/08-accountability-watchdog-lite/). Rubric: the six-question [Guardrail Stress Test](/levers/guardrail-stress-test/).

## The institution

- Name:
- Jurisdiction (city / county / state / federal):
- What it oversees:

## Selection

- Who appoints the leadership?
- Can one party complete an appointment alone? (Y/N)
- Term length, and can terms be cut short without cause? (Y/N)

## Budget

- Who sets the budget - statute on a multi-year cycle, or annual appropriations? (statute / annual)
- Has the budget been cut or frozen after unfavorable findings? (Y/N/unknown)

## Removal

- What does removing the leadership legally require?
- Is there an enforcement mechanism if that process is ignored, or only a notice requirement? (enforcement / notice only)
- Has anyone tested it? What happened?

## Visibility

- Can journalists and civic organizations inspect its work, or only receive official outputs? (inspect / outputs only)
- Are its reports published in full, on a schedule someone can check? (Y/N)

## The two summary tests

- **Opponent test:** would this design still be acceptable if political opponents controlled it? (Y/N)
- **Durability test:** does anything about this design bind the next government, or only describe the current one? (binds / describes)

## One ask

Based on the weakest answer above, write one specific ask - rule + owner + metric + deadline:

> Example: "Move the IG's budget to a two-year statutory cycle. Owner: state legislature (committee: \_\_\_). Metric: enacted before next session ends."

## Who to ask

- The institution itself (public records / press office)
- Your representative's district office
- Ballotpedia / state statute database for the legal structure
- If you can't get a straight answer, log that - it's a finding, not a dead end.
