# Contract Clause Snippets

Use these as plain-language starting points, not final legal text.

## Audit rights

The agency may review records, billing support, and performance documentation related to this contract on reasonable notice, and the vendor must retain those records for [X] years.

## Performance metrics

The vendor must report the following measures on a [monthly/quarterly] basis:

- [metric 1]
- [metric 2]
- [metric 3]

## Public reporting

The vendor will provide reporting in a format the agency can publish publicly without manual re-entry, except for legally protected information.

## Cure period

If the vendor misses a required performance threshold, the agency may require a written corrective action plan within [X] days, with follow-up reporting until the issue is resolved.

## Termination for non-performance

Repeated failure to meet material performance obligations, or failure to cure within the required period, is grounds for termination.
