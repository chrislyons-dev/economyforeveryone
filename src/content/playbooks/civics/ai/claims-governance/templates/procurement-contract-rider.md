# Procurement Contract Rider

Use this template for contracts involving AI-assisted denial, closure, disenrollment, or adverse determination systems.

## Minimum clauses

- specific reasons, not generic notices
- log retention and audit access
- claimant-accessible records
- appeal service levels
- reporting on denial, appeal, and overturn rates
- traceability from vendor output to final action
- prohibited AI-only final decisions for named high-stakes categories

## Accountability clause

The acting institution retains accountability for the outcome and cannot disclaim responsibility because a vendor supplied the output, score, or recommendation.

## Reporting clause

Vendor should provide regular reporting on:

- denial volume
- appeal rate
- overturn rate
- notice quality failures
- record retrieval performance
