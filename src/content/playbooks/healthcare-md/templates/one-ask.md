Use this when you need permission, a report, staffing, or an EHR/workflow change.

## The 6-sentence ask

Hello **[Name/Team]**,

1. I’m seeing **[specific admin drag]** causing **[impact]**.
2. The bottleneck appears to be **[constraint: rule, staffing, EHR friction, payer loop]**.
3. My request: **[one specific action]** by **[date]**.
4. Success looks like: **[metric]** improving by **[target]** within **[time]**.
5. Safety guardrail: **[what will not be automated / how humans stay accountable]**.
6. Who should own the next step, and when can we confirm it?

Thank you,  
**[Name]**
