# Call Your Rep Script (Voicemail)

Staff log calls as: **for/against + issue + district**.
Keep it short. One ask.

## The 30-second voicemail (template)

Hi, my name is **[Name]**. I’m a constituent in **[City/Neighborhood]**, ZIP **[#####]**.  
I’m calling to ask **[Rep/Senator/Member Name]** to **[support/oppose]** **[Bill/Policy/Action]**.  
**My one reason:** **[plain-language reason tied to squeeze/dignity/fairness]**.  
**Success looks like:** **[measurable outcome, optional]**.  
Please let me know the Representative’s position. You can reach me at **[phone/email]**. Thank you.

## Variant A — Vote YES

Hi, I’m **[Name]**, ZIP **[#####]**.  
Please vote **YES** on **[Bill # / item]**.  
This would **[one benefit]** and reduce **[monthly cost / time tax]**.  
Thanks for passing that along.

## Variant B — Vote NO

Hi, I’m **[Name]**, ZIP **[#####]**.  
Please vote **NO** on **[Bill # / item]**.  
My concern is **[one harm]** and it would make **[cost/time]** worse for families.  
Thank you.

## Variant C — Dignity + due process guardrail

Hi, I’m **[Name]**, ZIP **[#####]**.  
I’m calling to ask **[Rep]** to support **[action]** that protects **due process and human dignity**, and to oppose policies that rely on scapegoating.  
Please let me know the Representative’s position. Thank you.

## One-reason menu (pick one)

- “This reduces the monthly squeeze for working families.”
- “This makes the process predictable and fair.”
- “This improves reliability and lowers the time tax.”
- “This strengthens accountability with clear metrics.”
- “This protects dignity and due process.”
