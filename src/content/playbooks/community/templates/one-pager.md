# One-Pager Template (Community)

Use this as a handout or as your own clarity tool.
One page. One ask.

## Title
**[Place] — [Topic] — One Ask**

## 1) Problem (what’s happening)
1–2 sentences, plain language.

## 2) Mechanism (why it’s happening)
Name the constraint(s):
- timelines
- staffing
- rules
- contracts
- enforcement
- market power

## 3) Options (tradeoffs, short list)
- Option A: [what] — upside / downside
- Option B: [what] — upside / downside
- Option C: [what] — upside / downside

## 4) Recommendation (what good looks like)
State the principle and the reason.

## 5) One Ask (measurable)
**Ask:** [who does what by when]  
**Metric:** [how we measure success]  
**Reporting:** [how often it’s published] (optional)

## 6) What success looks like
A short, concrete outcome statement.
