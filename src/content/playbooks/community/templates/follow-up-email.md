**Goal:** confirm the record, get an owner + date, keep it calm.

## Subject line options

- “Follow-up: [topic] — next step + date?”
- “Thank you — confirming my understanding on [item]”
- “Quick follow-up: [ONE ASK]”

## Email (copy/paste)

Hello **[Title Lastname / Staff Name]**,

Thank you for **[meeting / call / reply]** on **[date]**.

My understanding is:

- **Ask:** **[one sentence]**
- **Next step:** **[what happens next]**
- **Owner:** **[name/office]**
- **Decision date:** **[date]**
- **Metric:** **[what will be measured/reported]**

If I have any of that wrong, please correct me.

If it’s right: is there anything you need from me (or the public) before **[decision date]**?

Thank you again,  
**[Name]**
