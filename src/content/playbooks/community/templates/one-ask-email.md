**Goal:** one clear request with a measurable outcome.

## Subject line options

- “Constituent request: [ONE ASK]”
- “Please support/oppose: [Bill/Item]”
- “Request for action at [meeting/date]: [ONE ASK]”

## Email (copy/paste)

Hello **[Title Lastname / Staff Name]**,

My name is **[Name]** and I’m a constituent in **[City/Neighborhood]**, ZIP **[#####]**.

I’m writing to ask **[Rep/Member/Department]** to **[support/oppose/do]** **[specific action]** by **[date/meeting]**.

**Why (one sentence):** **[plain reason tied to squeeze/dignity/fairness]**.

**Success looks like:** **[metric + timeline]**. (Optional but strong)

If you can’t support this exact step, what _can_ you commit to by **[date]**?

Thank you for your time. I’d appreciate a reply with the **yes/no** position or the right staff contact to follow up with.

Sincerely,  
**[Name]**  
**[Address or Neighborhood]**  
**[Phone/Email]**
