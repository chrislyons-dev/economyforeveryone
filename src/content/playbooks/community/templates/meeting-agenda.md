This is for a small group (or you + one ally).
Keep it action-oriented.

## 1) Check-in (3 minutes)

- What’s one thing you noticed this week about the squeeze?

## 2) Decide the target (5 minutes)

- Which squeeze lane are we touching this month?
- What’s the bottleneck we can actually move?

## 3) Pick the decision point (5 minutes)

- What meeting/vote/contract/budget moment is coming?
- Who is the decider? Who is staff?

## 4) Draft the One Ask (10 minutes)

- Who / what / when / metric
- What’s the “make it easy” next step?

## 5) Assign micro-actions (5 minutes)

- Who sends the email?
- Who reads the agenda packet?
- Who shows up / submits comment?
- Who logs it?

## 6) Close (2 minutes)

- Follow-up date:
- Metric to track:
