**Purpose:** reduce the monthly squeeze by reducing the time and stress tax of getting to work, school, and care.

## The mechanism (plain language)

Time is money. Unreliable transit and unsafe streets create hidden costs:
missed work, late pickups, more car expenses, higher stress.

## What good looks like

- reliable service (on-time)
- safer crossings and routes
- fewer bottlenecks
- small fixes that compound

## Quick wins (pick 1–3)

### Reliability (biggest leverage)

1. **Publish on-time and headway reliability** monthly (route-level).
2. **Bus priority at choke points** (signal priority, queue jumps).
3. **Stop consolidation** where stops are too frequent and slow the line.
4. **Dedicated maintenance response** for chronic breakdown hotspots.

### Safety (low cost, high impact)

5. **Fix top 10 crash intersections** with quick-build designs.
6. **Safe routes to school** improvements (crosswalks, flashing beacons, timing).
7. **Lighting and visibility upgrades** at stops and crossings.

### Access (practicality)

8. **Real-time information improvements** (signage, app reliability).
9. **Last-mile connections** (bike parking, sidewalk gaps near stops).
10. **Winter reliability plan** (snow clearance priorities for stops/sidewalks).

## Scoreboard metrics (choose 3–5)

- On-time performance (route-level)
- Headway reliability (for frequent routes)
- Average trip time on key corridors
- Crash/injury counts at top hotspots
- Sidewalk gap closures (count)
- Winter clearance response times

## Common failure modes

- Big capital plans with no near-term reliability wins.
- Metrics exist but aren’t published plainly.
- Safety treated as “education” instead of design + enforcement consistency.
- Winter operations not planned as a first-class system.

## Bridge language

- “Time is money. Reliability is affordability.”
- “We can’t ‘hope’ our way into safer streets.”
- “Let’s fix the top 10 bottlenecks and measure the change.”
- “Quick-build first, then permanent.”

## One Ask examples

- “Publish headway reliability monthly and improve Route X by 15% within 6 months.”
- “Fund quick-build fixes for the top 10 crash intersections and report outcomes quarterly.”
- “Adopt a winter reliability plan for stops/sidewalks with clear clearance priorities and metrics.”
