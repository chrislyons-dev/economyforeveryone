# AI Claims and Eligibility

This package is for the claimant-facing side of claims and eligibility failures: notices people can understand, records they can actually reach, and appeal paths that can still reverse harm in time.

Use it when the question is how a person, helper, navigator, or community institution makes a denial understandable and challengeable before it becomes effectively final.

## Folder map

- [Specific Reasons and Usable Notices](./specific-reasons-and-usable-notices.md)
- [Audit Logs, Records, and Appeals](./audit-logs-records-and-appeals.md)
- [Denial Notice Template](./templates/denial-notice-template.md)
- [Appeal Intake and Review Template](./templates/appeal-intake-and-review-template.md)

## Use this package for

- denial or closure notices that are too generic to challenge
- appeals that exist on paper but fail in practice
- records access that is too slow, partial, or hard to use
- community helpers building a practical defense path for claimants
