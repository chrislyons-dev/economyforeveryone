Pick **one**. Small counts.

## 1-minute actions

- Mute/unfollow one account that spikes your stress
- Text one friend: “You okay?”
- Write down: “What I can control / what I can’t”
- Put your phone in another room for 10 minutes

## 10-minute actions

- Look up one primary source together (school email, official site, full video)
- Take a short walk (no phones) and talk while moving
- Do a quick body reset: shower, stretch, snack, water
- Write one question you want answered (not ten)

## Weekly actions

- Do one community thing: club, helping a neighbor, volunteering
- Practice one “skills that age well” rep (writing, building, learning)
- Make one plan for a hard thing coming up (test, tryout, talk, deadline)

## If the fear is about “the world”

Choose an action that restores _local control_:

- one helpful act
- one skill rep
- one real conversation with a real person
